About

Hello, I'm Christine.

I write fiction that lingers in small moments — a kitchen table conversation, a train platform goodbye, the silence after a decision is made. My work has been described as tender, unhurried, and quietly observant.

When I'm not writing, I'm usually reading someone else's sentences twice, walking somewhere with no particular destination, or refilling a teacup that's already gone cold. This site is where I share my novels, short stories, and occasional thoughts on the writing life.
